SA Group Ltd — Elm website

Open index.html to view the site. Upload the HTML, CSS, JavaScript,
assets and robots.txt together, keeping the directory structure intact.

For deployment, run:
  python3 configure-domain.py https://YOUR-PUBLIC-DOMAIN
This sets absolute canonical and social URLs and builds the sitemap.
If the site is hosted under a subdirectory, include it in the URL.
Upload the updated site and sitemap after running the command.

Quote forms open an editable message in WhatsApp or the visitor's email
app. The visitor sends it there. No submissions are stored by this site.

Public URL, deployment and live social card verification are pending.

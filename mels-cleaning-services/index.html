BEFORE YOUR CLEAN

A few useful answers.

Where do you work?

Mels Cleaning Services covers Newcastle and surrounding areas. Send your postcode to confirm availability for your property.

What type of cleaning can I enquire about?

Domestic and commercial cleaning, including regular cleans, Home Reset and Deep Reset enquiries. Tell Mel what you would like included so the scope can be agreed.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Your postcode, property size, rooms to be cleaned, preferred frequency and any priority areas. Photographs can help explain what you need.

Are you DBS checked and insured?

Yes. Mels Cleaning Services describes the service as DBS checked and fully insured.

Does the form book my clean?

The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry for you to review and send. Mel will discuss the work and availability with you; it does not confirm a booking automatically.

CWL Landscaping Ltd works on outdoor spaces in Oxford. Garden projects in the portfolio combine paths, paved seating areas, lawn, planted borders and timber features. A useful starting point is the way you want to use the garden and the features that matter most to you.

Think about the garden as a connected space

Share how you move between the house, lawn, seating and other garden areas. Mention any parts that feel difficult to use or need a clearer layout, and tell CWL which existing plants and features you want to retain.

Include preparation in the conversation

Patios & Paving in Oxford

A paved area can connect the house with the garden, provide a place for seating or create a route between different spaces. CWL Landscaping Ltd undertakes patio and paving projects in Oxford, with examples ranging from pale rectangular paving to mixed natural stone colours.

Start with where you want to sit and walk

Tell CWL whether you want a patio beside the doors, a separate seating area or new paths through the garden. Include photographs of the current surface, access and any changes in level.

Consider paving and its edges together

Recent projects show paths around planted beds, paving alongside brick walls and patios adjoining lawn. Discuss the outline of the paved area, the surrounding borders and how it connects with the rest of your garden.

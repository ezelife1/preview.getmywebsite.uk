Questions

How do I make an enquiry?

WhatsApp is the quickest way. Include the preferred date, approximate time, how long you would like to meet and the type of arrangement you have in mind.

Do you take last-minute bookings?

You are welcome to ask, but availability cannot be guaranteed. Advance notice gives the best chance of securing the time you want.

Can I book for dinner or an event?

Yes. Dinner dates, social occasions, functions and other events are all suitable arrangements.

Can travel or overnight arrangements be discussed?

Yes. Longer bookings, travel and overnight arrangements can be discussed privately. Any travel, accommodation or related costs should be agreed before confirming.

Is everything confidential?

Privacy and discretion are central to the service. Only the information required to arrange the booking needs to be shared.

What if I am unsure exactly what I want?

That is completely fine. Explain the occasion or the kind of experience you are looking for and the options can be discussed privately before you decide.

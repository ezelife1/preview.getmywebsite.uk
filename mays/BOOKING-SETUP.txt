WHATSAPP BOOKING REQUEST FORM

No Calendly account or paid integration is required.
Customers complete the form and tap Send booking request on WhatsApp.
Their WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message addressed to May at +44 7512 121595.
Customers MUST tap Send in WhatsApp; no message is delivered by the website automatically.
May replies on WhatsApp to confirm or propose a different slot.
Requests are limited to Wednesday–Sunday, 08:00–20:00.
No live calendar availability is checked and this form does not prevent overlapping requests.

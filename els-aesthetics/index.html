BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A little
reassurance.

How do I book?

Message ELS on WhatsApp, call or email. We’ll discuss your chosen treatment, treatments and available appointments. An enquiry does not confirm a booking.

What if I’m not sure which treatment to choose?

Select “Help choosing a treatment” in the enquiry form. We can talk through the collection and arrange a suitability discussion.

Where is the studio?

ELS Aesthetics is at 72 Church Street, Gainsborough. Contact us before your visit if you need information about access.

Will my results look like the photographs?

Results and experiences vary from person to person. The photographs show individual clients and do not promise a particular result.

Can I discuss a health concern before booking?

Yes. Tell us you would like a private suitability discussion. Please avoid sending medical information through the website enquiry form.

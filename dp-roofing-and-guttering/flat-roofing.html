Flat Roofing & Extension Roofs

From the broad surface to the surrounding roof.

The flat roof projects include a dark roof over a rear extension, a roof viewed from above and a flat surface meeting brick walls. These views show the finished surface in context, including the relationship to adjoining pitched roofs and other parts of the property.

Extensions and adjoining roofs

An extension roof often sits close to walls, windows or an existing pitched roof. The portfolio shows different roof shapes and junctions, with both wide views and detail photographs. When you enquire, say where the roof is on the building and whether there are openings or adjoining roof sections.

An enquiry based on the actual roof

Send the approximate size, current roof covering if you know it, and the reason you are considering work. A photograph can help start the conversation, but the appropriate construction, finish and final scope need to be agreed for your roof. Ask about a free inspection to discuss the next step.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Which flat roof projects are shown?

The photographs show completed dark flat roof surfaces over extensions and a junction between flat and tiled roof areas.

Can I enquire about a leaking flat roof?

Yes. Describe where the concern appears, when you noticed it and the roof type if known. Include your postcode and photos taken safely.

Can you recommend a covering from a photo?

A photo starts the discussion. The roof condition and construction need to be considered before the covering and scope are agreed.

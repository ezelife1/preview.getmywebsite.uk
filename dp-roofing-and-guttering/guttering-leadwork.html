Guttering, Roof Edges & Leadwork

Roof edges, junctions and chimney details.

The roofline includes more than the main tile or flat roof surface. The photographs show guttering at the edge of a tiled roof, slate meeting a chimney and roof junctions. Ask DP Roofing & Guttering about the guttering or roof detail you want checked.

Chimneys and roof junctions

The slate roof photograph shows lead flashing around a brick chimney. Other views show hips and places where roof planes come together. If your enquiry concerns one of these areas, a wide photo helps locate the detail while a closer safe view can explain what you have noticed.

Guttering enquiries

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Can I ask about guttering as well as roofing?

Yes. Guttering forms part of the business service. Describe the affected run and include your location when you enquire.

Is chimney flashing shown in the portfolio?

Yes. One photograph shows lead flashing around a brick chimney on a slate roof.

Should I climb up to photograph the detail?

Use photos taken safely from ground level. Describe anything you cannot show clearly and discuss inspection access with the team.

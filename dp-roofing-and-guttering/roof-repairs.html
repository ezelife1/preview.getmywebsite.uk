Roof Repairs & Maintenance

Understand the concern. Agree the next step.

A roof concern needs to be understood before the work is agreed. DP Roofing & Guttering offers roofing repairs and maintenance, with free roof inspections and advice. Tell the team what you have noticed and which part of the property it affects.

Describe what has changed

Explain when you first noticed the issue and whether it follows rain or windy weather. If you know about earlier work to the area, include that too. Ground-level roof photos and a description of any visible internal signs can help the team understand the enquiry before discussing an inspection.

Repairs or wider roof work

Different roofs and conditions can need different work. The scope should follow the condition of the property, rather than a decision made from one isolated photo. You can use the free inspection to discuss the concern, the area that needs attention and the proposed work before accepting a quote.

Describe where it appears, when it happens and any roof work you know about. Add safe photos and the property postcode.

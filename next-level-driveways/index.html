BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Where do you work?

We welcome domestic project enquiries from across the UK. Send your postcode so we can discuss availability and the scope of your project.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes. Over 60 colours are available. Ask about samples and suitable combinations for your home, including contrasting borders.

Is resin bound permeable?

A resin bound surface can allow water to drain through when installed as part of a suitable permeable build-up. The base, drainage and site conditions need to be assessed for your property.

What should I send for a quote?

Include your postcode, approximate dimensions and a short description of the work. The form prepares a WhatsApp draft; you can attach photographs before sending it.

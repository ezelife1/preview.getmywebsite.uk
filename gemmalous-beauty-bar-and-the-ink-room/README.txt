GemmaLous Beauty Bar & The Ink Room — boutique website, studio edition with videos
Open index.html to view. Upload HTML, CSS, JS, robots.txt and assets together.

Before deployment, run:
  python3 configure-domain.py https://YOUR-PUBLIC-DOMAIN
Include any subdirectory in that URL. This creates the sitemap and sets
absolute canonical, Open Graph and social image URLs.

The Golf Smarter idea
You probably don't need more tips.

Golfers are surrounded by swing advice. One video says one thing, the next says another. Golf Smarter is about cutting through the noise and helping you understand what matters in your game.

Whether it's a simple chipping thought, a better way to practise or understanding why a shot keeps showing up, Tim's approach is practical: keep it clear, give it purpose and make it usable on the golf course.

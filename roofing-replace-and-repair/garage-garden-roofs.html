Separate buildings. A considered roof.

A detached building deserves the same attention to its roof as an extension on the house. The portfolio includes a flat roof on a timber-clad garden building and a grey pitched roof on a detached outbuilding. Ask about the roof on your garage, garden room or similar space.

Garden rooms and outbuildings

The timber-clad project shows a broad flat roof above glazed doors, while the detached grey building has a pitched roof. Roof shape, overhangs and the way rainwater leaves the building are useful details to include in your enquiry. Explain whether the building is already in use or part of a new project.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Do you work on detached buildings?

The portfolio shows both a timber-clad garden building and a detached outbuilding with a pitched roof. Send photos of your building to discuss the work.

Can I enquire about an existing garage roof?

Yes. Describe the roof, its approximate size and the reason for your enquiry. Include your postcode so coverage can be checked.

Which finish should I choose?

The suitable finish depends on the building and the roof construction. Discuss the intended use and current condition with Alex.

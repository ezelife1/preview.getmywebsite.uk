Garden Landscaping near Malpas & Whitchurch

A garden works best when its different areas feel connected. FAC Landscapes’ portfolio brings together paved seating spaces, lawn, planting areas and timber features. Whether you are changing one part of the garden or planning several improvements, describe how you want to use the space.

Start with the way you use the garden

Think about where you sit, how you move around the garden and the views from the house. Mention existing trees, planting and features you want to keep, along with any awkward corners or access that you want to improve.

Bring the different elements together

Curved paving edges, raised planting and timber features appear throughout the projects. Explain the wider plan when you enquire, including the lawn and boundaries, so the different parts of the work can be discussed together.

Patios & Garden Paving near Malpas

A patio can create a comfortable place beside the house or connect several parts of a garden. FAC Landscapes’ projects range from small stone setts around timber posts to broad pale paving and darker patios beside a garden room. Start with where you want to sit, walk and spend time.

Shape the patio around the garden

The portfolio includes curved edges beside lawns and paved areas outside glazed doors. Tell FAC how much room you need for seating and how the new patio should meet the lawn, planting and existing paths.

Discuss the surface and its surroundings

Bring together your ideas for the paving, borders and any steps or structures nearby. Views of the doors, boundaries and access routes will help explain the space and the connections that need to be considered.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

What services can I enquire about?

Landscaping, gardening, maintenance and handyman work. The project photos include patio paving, garden layouts and timber raised beds.

Where are you based?

North Eastern Landscaping is based in Scarborough. Include your postcode to confirm coverage and availability.

What helps with a quote?

Photographs, approximate measurements and a description of the work. Mention access, existing materials and what you would like to keep.

Can I ask about a new patio?

Yes. Share the size, preferred look and how you want to use the space. The portfolio includes stone paving and a patio refurbishment.

Can you help with raised beds?

The portfolio includes timber raised growing beds and gravel paths. Send your dimensions and ideas to discuss a garden project.

Can I enquire about smaller jobs?

Yes. Maintenance and handyman services are listed alongside landscaping and gardening. Explain the job so the scope and availability can be discussed.

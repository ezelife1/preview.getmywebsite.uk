Lawn Care

A neat lawn without the weekend work. SB Gardening offers mowing and edging for small and larger gardens, as a one-off visit or as part of a regular routine.

Lawn mowing

Small lawns up to 50m² are £20; medium lawns from 50–150m² are £30; lawns over 150m² start at £40.

Clean edges

Edging is listed at £10–£20 and includes lawn edges and borders. Ask about combining it with your mowing visit.

Regular visits

Fortnightly and monthly garden maintenance can include mowing, edges, weeding, a tidy and small shrub care.

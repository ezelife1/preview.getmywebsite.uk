Garden Tidy-ups

From a light refresh to a first-time tidy for an overgrown garden, choose the help your outdoor space needs.

A lighter tidy

General tidy-ups are £30–£45 for leaves, light weeding and a general tidy. Weeding borders, beds and paths is £20–£30.

Overgrown gardens

Small gardens up to 50m² are £120–£150, medium 50–150m² gardens £150–£180, and larger gardens £180–£250+. The package covers mowing, edging, trimming back, weed control and a full tidy; labour, tools and green waste disposal based on up to one tonne are included.

Green waste

Standalone green waste removal starts at £15–£20 for up to four bags. Grass, hedge, shrub and tree cuttings only. Larger loads are quoted using the supplied load price guide.

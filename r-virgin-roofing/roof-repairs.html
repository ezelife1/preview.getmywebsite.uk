Roof Repairs & Maintenance in Torquay

Understand the problem. Discuss the right work.

Roof repairs and maintenance form part of the R Virgin Roofing service. If you have noticed a concern, explain where it appears, when it started and which part of the building is affected. Photos and the property location help start a useful conversation.

Tell us what you have noticed

You do not need to diagnose the roof before getting in touch. Describe the change, any earlier work you know about and whether the concern appears after rain or windy weather. Take photographs only from a safe position and include a wider view of the roof if available.

Considering the existing roof

An isolated concern and a roof in poor overall condition can need different work. The appropriate approach depends on the roof itself.

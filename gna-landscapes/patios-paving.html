Patios & Garden Paving in Bristol

A well-placed patio creates a link between the house and the garden. GNA Landscapes installs patios and garden paving in Bristol, with projects showing natural stone colours, pale paving and darker grey finishes. The starting point is how much room you need and where you want to spend time.

Choose a layout around everyday use

Think about seating, doors opening onto the patio and the route to the rest of the garden. Send photographs of the existing space and tell us about any changes in level, narrow access or features you want to keep.

Consider the edges as well as the surface

Paving can meet lawn, planting, steps or a timber structure. The galleries show several of these connections. Discuss the outline of the patio and its surrounding areas alongside your preferred colours and finish.
